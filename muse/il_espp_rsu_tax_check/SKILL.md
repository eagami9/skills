# Check ESPP and RSU Tax Before a Sell and Rebuy

## Check realised gains first

- Read the established record for realised capital gains in the current tax year before you compute any saving from a sale.

- Treat a missing or unconfirmed gain as unknown and ask for the broker summary before you use it in arithmetic.

- State the verified realised gain figure and its source before you state any saving.

- Do not present a loss carryforward as a saving in the current year when there is no realised gain to offset.

## Verify the ESPP cost basis

- Check the broker summary for the ESPP purchase and read the market price on the purchase date.

- Treat the discounted purchase price as the price paid for the discount benefit, not as the capital gains cost basis.

- Measure the capital gain from the market price on the purchase date to the sale price, because the discount benefit is taxed separately as ordinary income through salary.

- Compare the first broker summary with any corrected summary and use the corrected one when the two disagree.

## Verify the RSU basis and holding period

- Check the grant date, the vesting date and the price for each RSU lot before you compute a gain.

- Check the date from which a preferential capital gains rate becomes available for that lot.

- Keep each lot separate when lots have different grant dates or holding periods.

## Compute the saving only from verified gains

- Compute a tax saving only from verified realised gains in the same year, using the rate from the broker summary or the user actual tax position.

- Split the tax move from the investment move and judge the sale on its own merits before you add any tax saving.

- Say plainly when there is no tax reason to sell now, even when the paper loss is large.

- Suggest a qualified tax adviser when the broker summary is unclear.
